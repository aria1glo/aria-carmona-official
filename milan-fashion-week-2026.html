Milan Fashion Week S/S27

Milan, ITA — September 28, 2026 — Aria Carmona's second foray into Europe resulted in a magnificent opener during RXR's Milan Fashion Week fall presentation. The 22 year old made amends from Paris Fashion Week by walking in Milan first time out, for three designers no less.

Like Miami earlier this year, Aria had generated a buzz earlier in the month - having racked up three New York Fashion Week shows, one of which being in collaboration with Rebel Agency. In the process, Aria becomes one of a very select few transgender American-Dominican women to lead a collection in the Milan classic. She also joins a narrow club in opening headline shows in Miami and Milan in the same year.

To add the cherry on top, Aria Carmona paid a visit to both Lisbon (Portugal) and the principality in Monte-Carlo in the days after the show for media and marketing duties. Monaco treated her very well, as the American model stated that "It's something I am extremely grateful for, Monaco has always been a bucket list location to go to in my career."

With a successful run in Europe complete and in the books, she looks to finish her year strongly as she hits the backend part of scheduled events. But for Carmona, her energy and morale as at an all time high, and she credits that to her agency owner at TDTD Models and Rebel Agency.
